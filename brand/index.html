Background Tolerance

Usage Rules

Always

Wolf first — the symbol identifies the brand alone.
Clear space ≥ 25% of wolf height on all sides.
Minimum size: 16px (favicon), 24px (inline), 88px (lockup).
Use the exact gradient stops; never rotate or skew the wolf.
Mono contexts: pure white or pure black wolf only.

Never

Do not replace the wolf with an abstract “A”.
Do not add a second competing symbol beside it.
Do not outline, shadow, bevel, animate parts, or add glow.
Do not recolor outside the defined palette.
Do not place the wolf on low-contrast mid-tone backgrounds.
